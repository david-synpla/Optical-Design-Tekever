# Track B working research brief

Use the preserved supplied notes as prior art, not a prescribed solution. Original requirements/eo_requirements.yaml is authoritative. No Track A prescription is a seed. reference/ remains inaccessible.

Screen the buy-versus-build trade first: stock SCT/reducer and photographic supertelephoto; retained commercial optics/custom mechanics; independently co-designed CDK and Cassegrain/relay; off-axis conic/aspheric TMA. Screen alternative families cheaply when their cost, spectral or fabrication trade warrants it. Do not infer a Pareto frontier from unmeasured image quality.

Independent checks on 2026-09-05:

- [Celestron C5](https://www.celestron.com/products/c5-spotting-scope) lists 127 mm and 1250 mm. [Reducer guidance](https://www.celestron.com/blogs/knowledgebase/understanding-focal-reducers) lists approximately 788 mm; [compatibility](https://www.celestron.com/products/reducer-corrector) includes C5. Nominal 0.63 reduction gives 787.5 mm. Do not substitute rounded catalogue figures for toleranced measurements.
- [Nikon specifications](https://imaging.nikon.com/imaging/lineup/lens/z-mount/z_800mmf63_vr_s/) confirm 800 mm, maximum f/6.3, 22 elements/14 groups, 5 m close focus, 140 x 385 mm and 2385 g. Published MTF is at 10/30 lines/mm, insufficient for 2.74 um detector assessment.
- [PlaneWave design description](https://www.planewave.eu/en/technologies/cdk-optical-design) supports the ellipsoid/spherical-secondary/corrector architecture. Commercial performance does not transfer to a new 128 mm design.
- The supplied COTS-adaptation, TMA review and LOROP papers remain briefing-level evidence: direct publisher retrieval returned 429 or an interstitial. No numerical claims from those papers are treated as verified simulation or qualification of our candidates.

Corrections to the briefing's suggestions: a Nikon lens at maximum f/6.3 cannot simply be commanded to f/6.2. Adjusting SCT reducer spacing cannot independently fix EFL and entrance pupil: at 794.203 mm and 127 mm pupil it is f/6.2536. No unspecified tolerance permits calling either exact compliance. A glass-containing imaging lens is not proven across 430–800 nm by photographic heritage alone. No mass, packaging, image quality or ruggedization limit beyond the stated temperature range is imposed.

Metrics: use the active array from pitch (11.22304 x 8.22 mm); a symmetric 3 x 3 full-field grid for screening and denser validation for developed designs. Sample 430, 500, 550, 650, 725 and 800 nm, including band edges. Equal weights are only a benchmark assumption pending spectrum/QE. Report MTF at 50, 100, 150 and 182.48 lp/mm, with a matched-pupil diffraction reference, plus 1/2/3-pixel ensquared energy. Clear-pupil Airy values are a shared baseline, not predictions for obstructed candidates. Use one common detector focus across wavelengths. Revalidate sampling, apertures, obstruction and full-field 800 m refocus before any freeze.

Economics: compare optical NRE, custom surface/metrology burden, structure, electronics and qualification separately. No fictional exact production costs; no purchases or vendor messages are authorized by this initialization.
