## Requirements and numerical findings

The requirements-only calculation gives 794.2029 mm EFL, 128.0972 mm pupil, f/6.2, active field 0.809645 x 0.593006 deg. The small difference from the rounded 0.81 deg input is recorded, not repaired by changing pixel pitch. Thin-lens 800 m refocus is +0.78923 mm; a real compound system needs ray-traced refocus. All candidates retain the flat IMX545. Operation from -25 to +50 C remains unverified for every candidate.

B01 nominal reducer gives 787.5 mm, f/6.20079, 3.47937 urad/pixel. Tuning to target EFL with a 127 mm pupil gives f/6.25357: spacing cannot solve both. B02 gives 800 mm, f/6.3, 3.425 urad/pixel; its maximum aperture cannot be opened to f/6.2 by control. These are close benchmarks, not exact compliance. B09 can use a 1.22185x relay with a 128.097 mm entrance stop; that relay and coma correction must actually be designed. B14 remains 800 mm even if stopped near f/6.2; electronic stop granularity does not prove an exact entrance pupil.

`metrics.json` and `diffraction_reference.png` report six wavelengths across 430–800 nm, MTF at 50/100/150/Nyquist and 1/2/3-pixel ideal ensquared energy. These are analytically normalized clear-pupil references, NOT simulated or measured candidate image quality. At 800 nm, Nyquist MTF is about 0.035 even for a perfect clear aperture. Developed obstructed designs need their own matched-pupil reference. Equal spectral weights are an assumption, not a measured radiometric model. No full-field/band compliance is inferred from catalogue MTF or architecture heritage.
