## Required analyses

For all three candidates, establish a common nominal baseline and then evaluate matched perturbations.

At minimum investigate:

### Powered-optic rigid-body errors

* axial despace
* X/Y decentre
* X/Y tilt where physically meaningful
* mirror clocking for B06 where relevant

### Detector errors

* axial focus displacement
* X/Y decentre if meaningful
* detector tilt

### Surface/manufacturing perturbations

Where supported credibly by the model:

* radius error
* conic/asphere error
* refractive-index/glass variation for B05
* lens thickness/spacing errors for B05

Do not invent production tolerances and then call them requirements.

Use initial diagnostic perturbations to determine **sensitivity slopes and relative robustness**. Clearly distinguish:

1. diagnostic perturbation magnitude,
2. measured sensitivity,
3. any subsequently proposed tolerance allocation.

### Compensators

Determine which realistic assembly compensators could recover performance, for example:

* detector focus
* detector tip/tilt if justified
* secondary axial position
* corrector-group axial position for B05
* selected mirror adjustments for B06

For each candidate report:

* uncompensated sensitivity;
* performance after realistic compensator use;
* number and type of compensators required;
* whether compensation is independent or strongly coupled;
* likely alignment/metrology difficulty.

Do not allow an unrealistic number of free compensators simply to rescue a candidate.

## Metrics

Use common field, wavelength and pupil sampling wherever possible.

At minimum track:

* worst and distribution of geometric RMS spot radius;
* MTF at 50, 100, 150 and detector Nyquist frequency where diffraction calculation is practical;
* 2x2-pixel ensquared energy;
* EFL/image-scale variation;
* vignetting/aperture survival;
* physical clearances;
* detector/refocus requirement.

For B05 and B06 diffraction comparisons, continue to compare each system against the appropriate matched physical-pupil diffraction reference. Remember that B05 is centrally obscured and B06 is unobscured.

Do not claim B06 is intrinsically superior simply because its ideal pupil has higher contrast; quantify how much of that advantage remains after perturbation.
