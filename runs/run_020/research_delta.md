# Run 020 research/market delta — 350 mm donor and SWIR coating branches

Evidence checked 2026-09-12:

- [Orion Optics UK 350 f/4.6 primary](https://www.orionoptics.co.uk/product/350-f4-6-primary/) lists a current retail range of GBP 1,264.92–1,840.32 excluding VAT across grade/secondary choices.
- The [Orion mirror catalogue](https://www.orionoptics.co.uk/product-category/optics/telescope-mirrors/) tabulates the nominal 350-class mirror as 353 mm diameter, 1600 mm focal length, 39 mm edge thickness and 8,280 g. It does not publish a guaranteed optical clear aperture on the reviewed page. Run 020 therefore treats >=311.371 mm usable figure as an unresolved supplier gate, not an established specification.
- The current [Orion elliptical-flat catalogue](https://www.orionoptics.co.uk/product-category/optics/elliptical-flats/) lists 63/75/82 mm minor-axis flats with 89/106/116 mm major axes and retail anchors of GBP 116/148/186 excluding VAT. Their Hilux coating is a visible-astronomy product; the supplier's [coating page](https://www.orionoptics.co.uk/optical-coatings/) describes almost 97% reflectivity over a wide visible region but supplies no verified 0.8–1.8 um requirement for these donor mirrors.
- The [Orion CT14 page](https://www.orionoptics.co.uk/product/ct14/) lists a 350 mm, 1600 mm OTA at about 26 kg and 1430 mm tube length. That is existence evidence for optics/cell technology only. No complete OTA is used as the UAV architecture or mass estimate.
- The [Edmund 317.5 mm parabolic example](https://www.edmundoptics.com/p/125-diameter-x-100-fl-uncoated-parabolic-mirror/2262) specifies only 286 mm clear aperture. It is excluded from the donor set despite its nominal diameter.
- The [Edmund protected-gold example](https://www.edmundoptics.com/p/75mm-dia-protected-gold-4-6lambda-mirror/6105/) specifies average reflectance above 96% over 700–2000 nm. This demonstrates a relevant coating class, not availability, price or qualification for a 353 mm curved donor.

The preferred C04 coating hypothesis is therefore **stock figured substrate plus separately qualified SWIR coating**, not “fully stock coated optic.” Stripping/recoating can change schedule, surface condition, figure verification, environmental qualification, warranty and supplier dependence. Factory supply of an uncoated or SWIR-coated figured mirror would be preferable if quoted, but no such quote or guarantee exists.

Catalogue prices are retail anchors only. They exclude custom correctors, SWIR coating, stripping, witness coupons, acceptance tests, lot continuity, export/shipping, UAV structure, integration and qualification. `reference/` was not accessed.
